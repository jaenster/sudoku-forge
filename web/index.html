The solver keeps a 9-bit candidate mask per cell and runs its propagators as a worklist until nothing changes: all-different on rows, columns, boxes and cages; cage sums against a compile-time table of digit combinations; thermometer bounds; and the 45 rule, which derives the sum of cells that stick in or out of any band of rows, columns or boxes. A clue set counts as a puzzle only when a search for a second solution fails.
